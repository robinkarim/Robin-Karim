## Section 4: Inventory Handling and Rotation Procedures

### 4.1 Purpose

This section establishes procedures for receiving, storing, and rotating supply inventory to maintain readiness and minimize shelf-life loss. It applies to all personnel with inventory handling responsibilities.

### 4.2 Receiving Procedures

Upon receipt, all inventory must be:

1. Verified against the shipping manifest for quantity and condition.
2. Logged into the inventory management system within the same operational day.
3. Inspected for damage or expiration date concerns before stocking.
4. Flagged immediately if any discrepancy is found between the physical count and the manifest; do not stock discrepant items pending resolution.

### 4.3 Storage Requirements

Items are stored according to their classification code, which determines temperature, humidity, and shelf-life handling requirements. Classification codes are printed on the item packaging and cross-referenced in Appendix C of the full manual.

### 4.4 Rotation Procedure (First-Expired, First-Out)

Inventory rotation follows a first-expired, first-out (FEFO) model rather than a simple first-in, first-out model, because shelf life varies by lot rather than by receipt date.

1. At each stocking cycle, personnel check the expiration date on incoming stock against existing stock in the same location.
2. Items with the nearest expiration date are positioned for pick-first access, regardless of which lot arrived most recently.
3. Any item within 30 days of expiration is flagged in the inventory system for priority use or disposition review.

### 4.5 Discrepancy Reporting

Any inventory discrepancy identified during receiving, rotation, or periodic audit must be reported through the standard discrepancy reporting form within 24 hours of discovery. Discrepancies are not to be resolved informally by adjusting counts without a logged report.
