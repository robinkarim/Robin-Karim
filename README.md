# Technical Writing & Business Systems Analysis Portfolio

Writing samples from 13+ years of contract technical writing and IT Business Systems Analyst work across cybersecurity, financial services, healthcare, energy, defense, and government sectors.

## About

This repository showcases deliverables demonstrating structured authoring, regulatory/compliance documentation, systems analysis artifacts, and AI-assisted documentation workflows. Samples are organized by domain to reflect the range of industries and document types I support as a technical writer and BSA.
