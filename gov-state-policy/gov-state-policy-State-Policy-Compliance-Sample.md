# State Agency Process Documentation
## Public Records Request Intake and Response Procedure

**Prepared for:** State Agency Records Office
**Purpose:** Document the standard intake-through-response process for public records requests, written to hold up under both internal audit and public-facing scrutiny.

---

### 1. Purpose and Authority

This procedure implements the agency's obligations for processing public records requests in accordance with applicable state public records law. It applies to all requests received by mail, email, or the online request portal.

### 2. Intake

1. All incoming requests are logged in the records tracking system within 1 business day of receipt, regardless of channel.
2. The requester receives an acknowledgment confirming receipt and the applicable statutory response window.
3. Requests are routed to the records custodian for the relevant program area based on subject matter.

### 3. Review and Response

1. The records custodian reviews the request and identifies responsive records within the statutory timeframe.
2. Records are reviewed for any applicable exemption (e.g., personal identifying information, records related to an active investigation) prior to release.
3. If an exemption applies to only part of a record, the record is redacted rather than withheld in full, and the redaction basis is documented in the file.
4. If the request cannot be completed within the statutory window, the requester is notified in writing of the delay and the reason before the deadline passes — not after.

### 4. Fee Determination

If reproduction or redaction work exceeds the threshold for a fee-free request, the requester is notified of the estimated fee before work proceeds, and given the option to narrow the request to avoid the fee.

### 5. Denial and Appeal Rights

Any full or partial denial must cite the specific statutory exemption relied upon and include information on the requester's right to appeal the determination. Denials are reviewed by the agency's legal office before being issued.

### 6. Recordkeeping

The agency retains a log of all requests, responses, and any denial basis for the retention period specified in the agency's records retention schedule, to support both internal audit and any future public inquiry into agency responsiveness.

---
*This procedure has been generalized and reflects the structure used in production state-agency process documentation. Specific statutory citations and agency names have been omitted or generalized.*
