# Environmental, Health, and Safety Compliance Documentation
## Hazardous Materials Storage and Handling — Well Site Chemical Inventory

**Regulatory Basis:** OSHA Hazard Communication Standard, applicable state environmental regulations
**Document Owner:** EHS Compliance

---

### 1. Purpose

Establishes requirements for the storage, handling, and inventory tracking of hazardous chemicals used in well site operations, ensuring compliance with hazard communication and environmental release-prevention requirements.

### 2. Inventory Requirements

All hazardous chemicals stored on-site must be:

1. Logged in the site chemical inventory with quantity, storage location, and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) on file.
2. Stored in a manner compatible with other stored chemicals — incompatible materials (e.g., oxidizers stored near flammables) must be physically separated per the compatibility chart in Appendix B.
3. Labeled per Hazard Communication Standard requirements, with no unlabeled secondary containers permitted on-site.

### 3. Secondary Containment

Any chemical storage area with potential for release must have secondary containment sized to hold a minimum of 110% of the largest single container's volume, to account for both the contents and any incidental rainwater accumulation in outdoor storage areas.

### 4. Inspection Schedule

- **Weekly:** Visual inspection of storage areas for container integrity, proper labeling, and containment condition, logged on the site inspection form.
- **Quarterly:** Full inventory reconciliation against the chemical inventory system, with discrepancies investigated and documented.
- **Annual:** Third-party compliance audit against current regulatory requirements.

### 5. Spill Response

Any release, regardless of size, must be reported to the site EHS coordinator immediately. Releases meeting the regulatory reportable-quantity threshold must also be reported to the applicable regulatory agency within the required notification window — this determination is made by the EHS coordinator, not by field personnel, to ensure the correct threshold and agency are applied.

### 6. Training

All personnel handling hazardous materials must complete Hazard Communication training, including SDS interpretation, before independent handling authorization, with annual refresher training thereafter.

### 7. Recordkeeping

Inspection logs, inventory reconciliation records, and training completion records are retained per the site's document retention schedule to support both internal audit and potential regulatory inspection.
