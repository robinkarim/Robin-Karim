# SOP and Process Documentation Writing Samples

Samples from Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and process documentation work, written through direct collaboration with the subject matter experts actually performing the work.

## What's in here

- **Standard Operating Procedures** — step-by-step procedures written for consistency across shifts, teams, or locations
- **Product and operating specifications** — combined spec-and-instruction content covering both what a product does and how to operate or maintain it
- **Compliance-driven process documentation** — including Environmental, Health, and Safety (EHS) content, written to satisfy regulatory review as well as day-to-day usability

## Background

This work comes from an oil and gas engagement producing EHS compliance documentation and bills of material, and a separate engagement producing product specs and operating/maintenance instructions through direct SME collaboration. Client-identifying details have been redacted or generalized.

If you need to see how this holds up in a regulated environment specifically, let me know — I can point you to the EHS compliance sample first.
