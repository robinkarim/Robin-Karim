# Business Systems Analyst Writing Samples

Samples from my work as an IT Business Systems Analyst, sitting between the business side and the technical build — translating stakeholder needs into documentation that developers, QA, and project leads can act on.

## What's in here

- **Business Requirements Documents (BRDs)** — scoped from stakeholder interviews and structured so both business sponsors and engineering teams can sign off on the same document, including traceability back to source interviews and tickets
- **Use cases** — written to walk a reviewer through a workflow end to end, including edge cases and exception paths
- **Gap and impact analysis** — comparing current-state and future-state processes, usually ahead of a system migration or platform change

## Background

Most of this work comes out of large-scale system migrations (student information systems, ITSM platforms, cloud migrations) where I ran 15–20 concurrent workstreams, conducted SME and stakeholder interviews directly, and owned the requirements documentation through each release cycle. Client-identifying details have been redacted or generalized.
