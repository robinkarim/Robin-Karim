# Cybersecurity and GRC Writing Samples

Samples from documentation work supporting cybersecurity programs and Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) initiatives — written in close partnership with security engineers and SMEs, as the writer who makes their technical decisions usable by the teams who have to act on them.

## What's in here

- **Security plans** — structured to satisfy both internal review and external audit, aligned to NIST Cybersecurity Framework and ISO 27001 conventions
- **Cyber response and recovery documentation** — incident-response and recovery procedures written so they're usable under pressure, not just accurate on paper
- **Risk and compliance content** — GRC narratives connecting a control or policy back to the standard it satisfies

## Background

This work comes out of a cybersecurity program at a large energy-sector client and a compliance-focused engagement in financial services, both run within Agile delivery teams. I worked directly with security engineers and compliance SMEs as the documentation lead — not as a certified security practitioner — translating technical and regulatory decisions into content non-specialists could follow. Client-identifying details have been redacted or generalized.

If you're evaluating this against a specific framework — NIST, ISO 27001, or otherwise — let me know and I can point you to the sample that lines up best.
