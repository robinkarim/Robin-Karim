# Requirements Validation and Determination Record
## Public Records Portal — Redaction Workflow Requirement

**Purpose:** Document how a requirement was gathered, validated, and finalized prior to publication, supporting the agency's audit trail for system requirements.

---

### 1. Requirement Statement

The records portal must allow a reviewer to apply a redaction to a specific portion of a document (not the entire document) and log the statutory basis for that redaction before the document can be marked ready for release.

### 2. Source

This requirement was raised during a stakeholder interview with the agency's records custodian team, following a finding that the prior process required withholding an entire record when only one section contained exempt information — resulting in requesters receiving less information than they were legally entitled to.

### 3. Validation Steps

1. **Interview confirmation:** Requirement was confirmed independently by two records custodians in separate interviews, reducing the risk that it reflected one individual's preference rather than a shared process gap.
2. **Legal review:** Agency legal counsel confirmed that partial redaction with documented basis is both permissible and preferred over full withholding, where technically feasible.
3. **Process walkthrough:** A walkthrough of 10 recent requests confirmed that 6 of the 10 would have qualified for partial rather than full release under the proposed requirement, validating that the change would have a measurable impact.

### 4. Determination

The requirement is approved as written and assigned to the portal redesign backlog as a must-have for the initial release, based on the legal review and the measurable impact shown in the process walkthrough.

### 5. Acceptance Criteria

- Reviewer can select a text range within a document and apply a redaction to that range only.
- System requires a statutory basis selection (from a controlled list) before the redaction can be saved.
- Redaction basis is retained in the record's audit log, visible to internal reviewers but not to the requester.
- A document with one or more redactions can still be marked ready for release; a document cannot be released with an unresolved (basis not yet selected) redaction pending.
