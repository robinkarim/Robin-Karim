# How to Update Your Contact Information in the Patient Portal

This guide walks you through updating your phone number, email, and mailing address in the patient portal. It takes about 2 minutes.

### Before You Start

You'll need to be logged in to the portal. If you're having trouble logging in, see [Resetting Your Portal Password] instead — this guide assumes you're already signed in.

### Steps

1. From the portal home screen, select **My Profile** in the top navigation.
2. Select **Edit** next to the Contact Information section.
3. Update any of the following fields: phone number, email address, mailing address.
4. Select **Save Changes**.
5. You'll see a confirmation banner at the top of the screen. If you updated your email address, check your new inbox for a verification link — your new email won't be active for portal notifications until you click that link.

### Common Questions

**Will this update my information with my insurance provider too?**
No. This updates the portal and your care team's records only. Insurance and billing contact information must be updated separately through your insurance provider.

**I updated my email but I'm not receiving the verification link.**
Check your spam folder first. If it's not there after 10 minutes, return to **My Profile** and select **Resend Verification** rather than re-entering the email address again.

**Can I remove a phone number entirely instead of replacing it?**
At least one phone number is required on file. To remove a secondary number, clear the field and save — the primary number field cannot be left blank.

### Still Need Help?

If you've followed these steps and still can't update your information, use the **Contact Support** link at the bottom of any portal page.
